Good to know

Questions, answered.

The site works locally in demo mode and switches to the real database and payment API when deployment credentials are added.

Can I pay by UPI or card?

Yes. Online checkout uses Razorpay, which can offer UPI, cards, netbanking, and supported wallets based on the merchant account.

Can I choose cash on delivery?

Yes. COD creates a confirmed order without opening the payment gateway.

How do I manage an order?

Open “Manage order” and enter the generated order ID with the mobile number used at checkout.

What happens when I cancel a paid order?

The site sends a cancellation request to the admin. A refund must then be processed through the payment provider.
